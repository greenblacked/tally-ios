Tally Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Tally is a personal budget app. It does not create an account, does not show ads, and does not send your data to a server.

What we store

Transactions and your savings goal are saved on your iPhone only, using the system storage on the device. If you delete the app, that data is removed with it.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email, or account
  • No location
  • No tracking or analytics
  • No third-party advertising

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the Tally iOS project.
